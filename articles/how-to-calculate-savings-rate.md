## How to improve the number in practice

- **Automate on payday.** A standing transfer to a separate savings account the day income lands removes willpower from the equation â€â€— the most reliable single change.
- **Increase raises deliberately.** When income rises, raise the automated transfer amount by about half of the increase before the rest reaches your spending account.
- **Count debt repayment as saving.** Paying extra on high-interest debt improves net worth at a guaranteed return; many planners count it in their rate.
- **Watch the denominator too.** The rate rises when spending falls. Cutting one recurring subscription can move a 5% rate to 6% with zero effort.
