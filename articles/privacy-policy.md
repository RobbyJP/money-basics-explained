# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

This page explains how Money Basics Explained ("this site") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit.

## Information this site collects

This site is a static website. It does not have user accounts, does not ask you to register, and does not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or payment details.

## Cookies and advertising

This site is a member of the Google AdSense program. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on this site.

- **Google's use of cookies**: Google's advertising cookies enable it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- **Opting out**: You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings (https://adssettings.google.com). You can also visit https://www.aboutads.info for information on how to opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising.

## Legal basis and your choices

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, this site shows a consent message when you first visit, giving you the choice to allow or decline cookies used for personalized advertising.

## Analytics

This site does not currently use third-party analytics or tracking beyond the advertising cookies described above.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

## Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please contact us via the channel listed on the site's contact information.
