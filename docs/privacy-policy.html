Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 12, 2026

This page explains how Money Clarity ("this site", accessible from https://moneyclarity.blog) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit.

1. What this site collects

This site is a static website. It has no user accounts, no registration, and no checkout. We do not collect your name, email address, or payment details, and we do not have access to server access logs.

All values entered into the site's interactive calculators (APR vs APY, Compound Interest, Debt Payoff, Dollar-Cost Averaging, Emergency Fund, Expense Ratio) are processed entirely in your web browser. Nothing you type into a calculator is sent to us or stored anywhere.

The contact form is provided by a third-party form service (FormSubmit) purely to forward your message to us by email; the message contents are transmitted to that service and to our email inbox. See that service's own privacy policy for its practices.

2. Cookies and advertising (Google AdSense)

This site participates in the Google AdSense program and may display advertisements.

  • Google's use of cookies: Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • DART cookie: Google, as a third-party vendor, uses the DART cookie to serve ads to users based on their visits to this site and other sites on the Internet.
  • Personalized advertising: Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to Money Clarity and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Opting out: Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Third-party privacy policies

This Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or websites. We advise you to consult the respective privacy policies of these third-party ad servers for more detailed information, including their practices and instructions about how to opt out of certain options.

4. Your rights (EEA, UK, and California)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or California, you have specific rights under applicable data protection laws (GDPR and CCPA/CPRA):

  • Right to access / know: You have the right to request information about what personal data is held about you and how it is processed.
  • Right to deletion / erasure: You have the right to request deletion of your personal data under certain conditions.
  • Do Not Sell: We do not sell personal information to third parties.
  • Consent choice: If advertising cookies are used, a consent message is shown on first visit so you can allow or decline cookies used for personalized advertising.

5. Financial disclaimer

All content published on Money Clarity — including articles, guides, and calculator results — is provided solely for educational and informational purposes. It does not constitute professional financial, tax, investment, or legal advice. See the disclaimer for details.

6. Changes to this policy

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us through the contact page.
